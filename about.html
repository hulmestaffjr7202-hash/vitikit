🔬 About VitiKit

Built by plant-science researchers · Hosted free · No tracking

VitiKit started inside a university precision-agriculture lab working on machine-vision canopy sensing and laser vineyard management (our team holds a patent application on intelligent laser shoot-thinning for wine grapes). While building crop models we kept re-deriving the same field numbers — GDD sums, Winkler classes, tank mixes, frost odds — and decided to ship them as clean, fast, ad-light tools for working growers.

Principles

  • Free forever for the four core calculators
  • Sourced methods only — every formula cites peer-reviewed work in the guides
  • No accounts, no trackers; all computation runs in your browser
  • Pro modules later: weather-station auto-import and PDF spray records will fund hosting

Contact & feedback

Tell us what's missing or broken — especially with real-world numbers from your block. Email: vitikit.app@gmail.com. Feature requests welcome; growers' data stays yours.

VitiKit provides educational calculators, not agronomic advice. Always follow local regulations and product labels.
