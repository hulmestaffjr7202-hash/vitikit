Tip: no weather station? Free daily data: Open-Meteo API (open-meteo.com), NOAA CLI. The single-sine method (Snyder, 1985) is the viticulture standard — more accurate than averaging on cold nights.

Why it matters: Grapevine development is temperature-driven, not calendar-driven. Flowering at ~250 GDD°C and véraison near 1000 GDD°C let you schedule leaf-pulling, sprays, harvest crews and tank space weeks ahead.
