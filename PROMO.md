**标题：**
I built a free toolkit for the numbers we all run every season (GDD, Winkler zones, spray mixes, frost risk)

**正文：**
Hi everyone — I'm a plant science researcher working on precision viticulture (machine-vision canopy management). Every season I watch people redo the same math: single-sine GDD accumulations, Winkler classifications, tank mix conversions from label rates, and "is tonight a radiation frost?" checks.

So I packaged those four calculators into one free site: [链接]

- Single-sine GDD (base 10°C) with phenology stage prediction
- Winkler Region I–V classifier with variety suggestions
- Spray tank mixer (label rate → product per your actual tank)
- Frost risk scoring from dewpoint/wind/cloud

No signup, no tracking, everything runs in your browser. The methods are the standard ones (Coombe '95, Winkler et al. '74, Snyder's single-sine).

It's free and I plan to keep the core that way. If you try it, tell me what's missing or wrong — especially if your block's real numbers don't match. That feedback is worth more to me than money right now.
