Why free?

VitiKit is built by university plant-science researchers working on precision viticulture (laser canopy management, machine vision crop modeling). The calculators are our way of giving back to the grower community — and staying sharp on the real-world numbers. Pro modules (weather-station import, PDF spray records) fund the hosting.
